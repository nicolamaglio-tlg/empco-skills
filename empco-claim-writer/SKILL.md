# EmpCo claim writer

Turn what someone wants to say into claim directions that carry less EmpCo risk, and spell out the evidence each direction needs. The output is a brief for a marketer and their legal reviewer, not cleared copy — never describe a direction as "compliant" or "approved", and say so in every output.

## 1. Understand the starting point

The input is one of three things:

- **A flagged claim** — existing wording judged risky, by empco-screener or anyone else.
- **A fact** — something the business did or measured ("we cut packaging weight by 30%", "we fund restoration on 400 ha").
- **An aspiration** — what they'd like to be able to say ("our shoes are good for the planet").

Then establish, briefly:

1. **What it's about** — the product, range, or business, and which part of it the claim concerns.
2. **Audience** — default to EU consumers.
3. **Channel** — packaging, ad, webpage, social. It decides how much room there is for the qualification that has to travel with the claim.
4. **Evidence already held** — what they have measured, certified, or published.

Don't interrogate. If there's enough to work with, proceed, state your assumptions, and list the open questions in the data checklist.

## 2. Check what's off the table

Read `references/empco-screening.md`. If the idea falls into a blacklisted practice — a product-level "climate neutral" claim based on offsetting, a self-created sustainability badge, a generic claim like "eco-friendly" without recognised excellent environmental performance, a whole-product claim from one component, a legal requirement presented as a distinctive benefit — say so up front and explain why no rewording rescues it. Then look for what *can* be said about the same underlying fact.

## 3. Build directions

Read `references/writing-guide.md`. Produce two to four directions, ordered from most conservative to most ambitious. For each: example wording, the scope and qualifications that must travel with it, the evidence it requires, red lines it must not cross, and the risk that remains.

## 4. Output

Use `assets/brief-template.md`. End with a consolidated data checklist — every piece of evidence needed across all directions, phrased as questions the marketer can take to product, sustainability, or supply-chain teams. Close with the note that these are drafts for legal review, not cleared copy.
