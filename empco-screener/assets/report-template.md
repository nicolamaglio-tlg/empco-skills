# Report templates

Pick the form by size: **quick check** for a single claim or a short ad/post, **full register** for a webpage, a PDF, or copy with several claims.

---

## Quick check

> First-pass screening only. Not legal advice or legal clearance.

**Claim:** "exact wording" (or a description of the visual)
**Input:** text / image / … — reviewed: text only, or text + visuals
**Priority:** high / medium / low — **Bucket:** potential prohibited pattern / context-dependent issue / insufficient information

**Confidence:** high / medium / low — **Human review:** required / optional
**Why:** factual reason, grounded in the exact wording.
**What would change this:** the context or evidence that would lower (or raise) the risk.
**Next action:** …

If flagged, offer lower-risk directions via empco-claim-writer.

---

## Full register

> First-pass screening only. Not legal advice or legal clearance.

### Input

- Item: URL / file name / "pasted copy"
- Type and how it was read: e.g. Firecrawl fetch, PDF read natively (pages 1–12), image reviewed directly
- Audience:
- Failures or retries:

### Relevant material

Everything that describes or promotes the product or brand — the full candidate set, before filtering to claims. Quote exactly; describe visuals precisely.

### EmpCo assessment

| Priority | Exact claim | Bucket | Category | Why flagged | Evidence/qualification nearby | Missing information | Next action | Confidence / human review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Order rows by priority, high first. Medium- and high-priority claims get a row each; similar low-priority items may share one.

If nothing qualifies, leave the table out: say so in one sentence, then list what was checked and ruled out, and why.

### Limitations

- What was reviewed: text only / text + visuals.
- Scope: this item only — says nothing about other pages, locales, documents, or the business generally.
- Anything confirmed as out of scope (pages skipped, non-EmpCo commercial claims noted but not assessed).
